Behind the Camera

I'm Sawan, a wedding cinematographer who believes the best films are the ones that disappear into the day itself.

I focus on genuine emotions, natural moments and the small, unscripted details that make every wedding different — building each film around the way your day actually unfolded rather than a fixed formula.

Every couple, every family and every ritual is treated with care, so the film you receive feels honest to the day you lived.
